# Security policy for this organization

This is the organization default. A repository with its own security policy follows that policy instead.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report a suspected vulnerability privately. Do not open a public issue or a discussion for it.

1. Where the affected repository provides private vulnerability reporting, open its **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and fill in the form.
2. Otherwise, email **security@powerbrowser.org**.

Include the affected project and version, the steps that show the problem, and the impact you expect. Do not send passwords, tokens or private keys. Describe their kind and where they appeared, and leave the values out.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge your report within 5 business days.
- We tell you whether we can reproduce it, and what we plan to do.
- We tell you when a fix ships. We do not promise a fix date in advance.
