# Code of conduct

The projects of this organization are open to everyone who wants to help. We expect everyone who takes part, in issues, discussions, pull requests and any other project space, to be respectful and to focus on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good intent, and ask for facts before drawing a conclusion.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Keep discussion of a security problem in the private channel described in the security policy.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or demeaning comments, in any form.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Repeatedly disrupting a discussion after a maintainer has asked you to stop.

## Reporting

Report a concern to **contact@powerbrowser.org**. Say what happened and where. A maintainer reads each report, and replies with what happens next. Reports are handled in confidence, and the reporter is not named to the person reported.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close an issue or pull request, or limit a person's participation, in proportion to the problem. A decision can be appealed by writing to the same address.
