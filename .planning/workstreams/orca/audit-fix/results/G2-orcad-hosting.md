It is async, never throws, and resolves `{ tcp: string, tcp6: string, uid: number|null }`. My side reads both tables since 4a68e4f4. On Linux, `tcp` and `tcp6` are the UTF-8 texts of `IOUtils.read("/proc/net/tcp")` and `IOUtils.read("/proc/net/tcp6")`, each `{ maxBytes: 1 << 20 }`, the explicit maxBytes that readProcessStartTicks already uses for procfs, and `uid` is the first number on the `Uid:` line of `/proc/self/status`. Elsewhere, or on any failure, it resolves `{ tcp: "", uid: null }`.
