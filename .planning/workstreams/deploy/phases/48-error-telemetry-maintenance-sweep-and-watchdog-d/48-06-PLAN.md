## Objective

(48-REVIEW.md IN-03, reproduced with `{path:'/home/alice/x', user:'alice'}`). The Worker drops
usage events, but the data has already left the machine. Owner ruling (FINISH-PLAN section 2):
apply the admitted-key and shape filter to the usage path; keep the TEL-06 preference text.
