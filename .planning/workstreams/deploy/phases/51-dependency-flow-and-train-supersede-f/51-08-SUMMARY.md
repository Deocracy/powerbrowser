# 51-08 summary: Theia licensing regeneration on the runner

**Superseded in part by 51-09 (2026-11-04):** the single job below ran the branch's own scripts next to the App
token. 51-09 splits it into a credential-free generate job and a publish job that runs only main's code; see
51-09-SUMMARY.md and BLOCKED.md item 18.

## What changed

- `.github/renovate.json5`: `postUpgradeTasks` removed; the header says why and names the replacement job.
- `.github/workflows/release-train.yml`: `renovate-theia` no longer sets `RENOVATE_ALLOWED_POST_UPGRADE_COMMANDS`. New job
  `renovate-licensing` (needs derive, renovate-theia; job gate `!inputs.dry-run && needs.renovate-theia.result == 'success'`;
  environment bot; `permissions: {}`; `continue-on-error: true`; env `BRANCH: chore/theia-theia`). Steps: App token
  (contents write), checkout (fetch-depth 0, no persisted credentials), Nix, then on the branch: fetch-upstream.sh and
  apply-patches.sh, `yarn install --ignore-scripts --frozen-lockfile`, `generate-licensing.mjs`, commit only the two
  notice files (any other change fails the step), and `publish-bump-branch.sh "$BRANCH" "$BASE"`. Absent branch or
  current notices end with a NOTE; a refusal ends with a NOTE asking for a hand regeneration.
- `scripts/verify-renovate-config.mjs`: rule `licensing-hook` replaced by `no-post-upgrade`; `licensing-inputs` now covers
  the generator and its two outputs; 53 plants.
- `scripts/verify-workflows.mjs`: rule `renovate-job` forbids the allow-list on any Renovate step and requires exactly one
  licensing job per Renovate job with the shape above, the branch derived from `branchPrefix` plus the theia
  `groupSlug`. 12 renovate-job plants; 224 plants in total.
- `docs/RELEASING.md` Theia inflow paragraph; BLOCKED.md items 6 (superseded) and 18 (resolved).
