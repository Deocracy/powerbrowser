Press-and-hold: Back and Forward get `onPointerDown` (primary button only) starting a 500 ms timer that opens `openHistoryMenu` and sets a suppress-next-click flag; `onPointerUp`, `onPointerLeave` and `onPointerCancel` clear the timer; their click handler consumes the flag and returns without navigating (the release after a hold does not navigate). Disabled buttons receive no pointer or click events in Gecko, so the menu starts only from enabled buttons.

650 ms guard (UI-SPEC rule, stricter than Firefox on purpose, Pitfall P7): the state subscription remembers the previous `loading` of the subscribed tab; on a true-to-false flip it records `performance.now() + 650`; `onReloadClick` returns early while not loading and before that time. No dimming.

Shift+click: `onReloadClick` calls `tab.reload(true)` when `e.shiftKey` (no distinct glyph or tooltip).

Middle-click and Ctrl+click (Open Question 6: a URL-only duplicate): inject `WebTabOpenHandler` and `GroupModel`. Add `protected async openInNewTab(which: 'back' | 'forward' | 'reload')`: Reload uses the current tab's URL when it has an http(s) page; Back and Forward read `tab.history()` and take the entry at `reply.index - 1` or `reply.index + 1`; then `openInBackground(url, tab)`, then `groups.joinActiveGroup(widget)` in a try/catch that logs `[@powerbrowser/chrome-bar] joining the active group failed:` with the error name only. Back, Forward and Reload get `onAuxClick` running it for `e.button === 1`; their click handlers run it instead of navigating when `e.ctrlKey || e.metaKey`. Middle-click on the Stop state does nothing. The current tab stays selected and focused.
