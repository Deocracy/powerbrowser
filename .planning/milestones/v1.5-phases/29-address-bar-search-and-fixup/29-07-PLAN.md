<objective>
Make an open-tab result switch to that tab instead of loading it again (BAR-06), and give typed text and every row Firefox's new-tab gestures: Alt+Enter to a foreground tab, Ctrl+click and middle-click to a background tab (BAR-08).

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 3's first two clauses. Decided (autonomous, recommended; 29-UI-SPEC "New-tab targets"): Alt+Enter and Ctrl/middle-click on an open-tab row open its URL in a new tab instead of switching; the row's caption does not change while a modifier is held. The foreground tab is placed with `openInBackground(url, current)` and then activated, so both gestures put the tab directly after the current one (`openUrl` appends at the end).

Output: the Switch to Tab caption and behaviour, the two gestures, live scenarios `switch-to-tab` and `new-tab-targets`.
</objective>
