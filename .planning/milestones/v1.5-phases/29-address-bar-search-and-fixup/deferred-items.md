## From 29-09

- **one-off flake: likely cause found and fixed in the harness.** The one-off ArrowDown loop read the strip straight after each key press, before the re-render, so it could count one press late and leave the highlight on the button after Probe Engine Two. Enter then searched with a different engine, which matches the 29-08 symptom ("no GET on /two/search ... after Enter on the highlighted one-off"). In 29-09's first full run the same race showed as "ArrowDown past the last row never highlighted the Probe Engine Two one-off". Each press now waits for the highlight to move (`highlightOf` plus the strip state) before reading it. After the fix, `--scenario search,one-off` passed 3 of 3 runs. The new shift-del and context-menu steps use the same wait.
- **context-menu, focus after Paste & Go: intermittent in sessions that ran token-cookie first.** Paste & Go loads `/pasted` in the session tab every time (a GET, a loaded push), but chrome's focus sometimes does not end in the tab's page. Rates in 29-09: about 2 of 5 full runs, and 2 of 4 runs of `--scenario token-cookie,search,context-menu`. It never failed in 8 runs that did not include token-cookie (`search,context-menu` and `search,canonize-drop,shift-del,context-menu`). Evidence from instrumented runs:
  - First widget version (focus sent at once): the overlay `<browser>` got focus, then lost it 32 ms later with no related target. The window stayed active and the overlay stayed visible.
  - Final widget version (focus sent once the load has finished): the pushes were `about:blank` loading, then `/pasted` loaded, then `webTabFocus` was sent. Chrome then logged no focus event at all, and its active element stayed the Theia frame.
  - The `about:blank` push means the overlay got a fresh document, that is, a content-process switch. This looks like the Phase 27 deferred GUI-02 remoteness defect (see that phase's deferred-items), which leaves a switched overlay unable to take focus. Fixing it means changing how `PowerBrowserAPI.webTabOpen`'s overlay handles a remoteness change, which is out of scope here. The live check still fails the step when it happens; the step was not weakened.
