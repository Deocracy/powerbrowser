The suggest endpoints and the Wikipedia search path are the ones Firefox's own config uses; the DuckDuckGo Lite search URL is assumption A4.

Output: the manifest table and its schema rows, the generator's validation, emitter and frozen row, the tracked policy key, three allowlist rows, the guide rows, `scripts/verify-search-engines.mjs`.
