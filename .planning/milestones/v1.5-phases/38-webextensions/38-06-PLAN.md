<objective>
Gap closure for three 31.1 security findings, by owner decision (Chris, 2026-11-01), before
Phase 38 ships extension install.

- UF-31.1-02 (high): network observers see the Theia frame's requests and the POWERBROWSER_TOKEN
  cookie. Fix in a new patch, 060: every webRequest listener, DNR, and proxy.onRequest skip a
  channel whose top-level browser element is marked `extensionnetworkhidden`; the shell marks the
  Theia frame. browser.cookies hides the cookies the embedder lists in the
  `extensions.cookies.hidden` pref; the shell lists the token cookie.
- UF-31.1-01 (medium): extensions can close or resize the shell window past quitApplication()
  and the unsaved-work veto. Fix in 050: windows.remove and the state, geometry and titlePreface
  fields of windows.update are refused on a tab host window.
- UF-31.1-03 (medium): already closed by b38ab95 (scope gate family `messagemanagergroup`,
  plants at the XHTML and the JS write). Re-checked here; no new rule is needed.
</objective>
